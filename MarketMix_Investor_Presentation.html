Role-based workspaces for landlords, agents, drivers and administrators to manage listings, requests and service steps.

Product workflow03

How it works

Purpose-built roles keep each handoff visible.

Renter or buyer

Searches homes, compares details, saves options, contacts a listing party and arranges a viewing.

Landlord or agent

Submits listings for review, manages property information and follows up on customer interest.

Driver

Registers a vehicle, service area and carrying capacity; reviews eligible offers and trip activity.

Administrator

Reviews supply, handles service requests and supports cases where automated driver matching has no fit.

Additional product areas include roommate discovery, short stays, guided site-seeing, mortgage estimates, campaigns, messaging and customer support.

People and workflows04

Trust and service quality

The marketplace earns trust through reliable operations.

Operating standards to build

  • Current listing availability, prices and contact authority.
  • Clear viewing, transport, payment and cancellation terms.
  • Visible ownership of leads and timely responses.
  • Documented escalation for incorrect listings or service failures.
  • Payment reconciliation and auditable administrative decisions.

What the product foundation includes

Firebase authentication and data storage, an Express backend, payment initiation integrations, messaging workflows, listing moderation and role-based screens.

Integration code indicates technical paths; it does not prove production uptime, partner approval, successful delivery or regulatory clearance.

Business model

Several revenue paths can be tested against customer value.

Listing services

Optional paid promotion, verified listing support or higher-volume tools for landlords and agents.

Test willingness to pay

Viewing services

Disclose inclusions and refund terms

Moving services

A disclosed platform commission or referral/service fee on completed, paid transport jobs.

Measure contribution margin

Campaigns and partnerships

Sponsored property tours, relevant local campaigns or partner services with transparent placement.

Pilot with clear outcomes

These are monetization opportunities, not claims of current revenue. Payment code, default prices or commission settings do not establish sales, settlement or profitability.

Monetization hypotheses06

Go-to-market

Start with density and service reliability in a focused area.

Supply

Build dependable inventory

Concentrate on selected neighborhoods and property types. Confirm availability and listing authority.

Demand

Acquire high-intent users

Use local partnerships, referrals and targeted campaigns; measure qualified inquiries, not just visits.

Service

Close the loop

Track responses, attended viewings, completed moves, complaints and repeat usage before expanding coverage.

Potential early measures: active verified listings · inquiry response time · inquiry-to-viewing conversion · viewing attendance · move completion · repeat use · refund rate · contribution margin per completed service.

Proposed validation path07

Evidence and readiness

Investor diligence should separate product capability from traction.

Demonstrable in the product

Property discovery, account roles, listing workflows, viewing and booking screens, transport request flows, driver tools and administration interfaces.

Requires operating evidence

Current active inventory, customer growth, conversion, revenue, partner coverage, payment settlement, retention, satisfaction and unit economics.

Requires production checks

Live backend deployment, Firestore security rules, messaging delivery, payment webhooks, Android signing and end-to-end service reliability.

Keep demo data labeled

Investor dashboard sample returns and placeholder homepage counts are not company performance metrics.

Evidence discipline08

The investment discussion

Back the next proof points, not unverified scale claims.

Capital can accelerate

Focused inventory acquisition and verification, local operations, customer support, product reliability, provider onboarding, analytics and carefully measured customer acquisition.

Use a milestone-based plan

  • Choose an initial geography and customer segment.
  • Set measurable supply and response standards.
  • Run a defined pilot for viewings and moving.
  • Reconcile every payment and service cost.
  • Expand only when service quality and margins support it.

Funding amount, valuation, current traction and forecasts should be added only after management supplies verified, dated records and an agreed operating plan.
